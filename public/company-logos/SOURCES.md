# Company badge artwork

These files are original company assets, bundled for clear and reliable frontend badges. They are not generated or traced replicas. Company trademarks belong to their respective owners.

| Local file | Original source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tata.png | https://www.tata.com/content/dam/tata/images/favicons/apple-touch-icon.png | Original 180 × 180 Tata group mark; used for Tata Motors and Tata Steel, which already used the Tata mark in the application. |
| tcs.png | https://www.tcs.com/etc.clientlibs/tcs/clientlibs/clientlib-resources/resources/images/tcs_favicon_48.png | Original 48 × 48 company icon. Raster rendering is capped at its available pixel density. |
| reliance.png | https://rilstaticasset.akamaized.net/sites/default/files/2022-09/logo-scroll.png | Original 49 × 44 company mark from Reliance's website. Raster rendering is capped at its available pixel density. |
| hdfcbank.svg | https://s7ap1.scene7.com/is/content/hdfcbankPWS/hdfc-bank-logo?fmt=webp | Original vector paths unchanged. The SVG viewport selects the existing square bank emblem (3 3 28.67 28.67) for compact badges. |
| icicibank.ico | https://www.icici.bank.in/favicon.ico | Original 48 × 48 company icon. |
| itc.png | https://itcportal.com/content/dam/itc-corporate/ITC-Logo-Updated.png | Original 97 × 110 company logo. |

Other symbols retain the existing remote-provider and initials fallbacks. Image load handling skips successful but undersized (<32px) favicons and avoids enlarging accepted raster images beyond their intrinsic size at the current device pixel ratio. Vector artwork can scale freely.
